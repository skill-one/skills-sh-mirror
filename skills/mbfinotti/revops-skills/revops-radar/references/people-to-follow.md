## How to use this list

- Match the "Best followed for" line to the user's named themes; a depth-focused user gets 3-5 people on their themes, not the whole table.
- Prefer Independent entries when the user excluded vendor-run sources; vendor- and employer-affiliated people can stay if flagged, since affiliation shapes what they publish but does not disqualify their expertise.
- To find people beyond this table, work the surfaces in order of names found per minute of digging: `podcast guest lists == conference speaker rosters > community team pages > vendor benchmark posts`. The first two tie because both are dated, fetchable rosters of people someone already vetted - start with whichever the user's segment is closer to. Vendor posts come last: they do cite independent practitioners, but one name at a time.
