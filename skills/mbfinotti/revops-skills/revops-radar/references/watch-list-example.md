# Worked example - personalised RevOps watch list

## Persona

- Sofia, RevOps **manager** at a B2B SaaS scale-up
- Time budget: **90 min/week**
- Building a **standing habit**, not catching up
- Mediums: audio + text
- Wants **breadth**
- Ruled out: paid peer communities, no employer budget for one
- Vendor-run sources: include, flagged
- Stack anchored on a dominant CRM platform: yes, include ecosystem sources
- Podcasts consumed while commuting (podcast cost halved to ~20 min of dedicated attention)

## The watch list (good example)

### Bench (verified substitutes)

Common Room blog (active, vendor-run) · RevOps FM (stale at check, ~7.5 mo - re-check next refresh, swap in if it resumes)

### Dropped (ruled out)

Pavilion - paid membership, no employer budget. Deleted, not benched: a source she cannot buy would sit on the bench looking available and get re-proposed every quarter.

**Totals: ~85 of 90 min/week · 6 weekly + 2 monthly + 1 event · 8 of 9 verified-active, 1 unverified user-accepted.**

### Where the efficiency order was overridden

Pure value-per-minute would have filled all 90 minutes with newsletters and vendor blogs, and Sofia would know every headline and no reasoning. Two long-form entries were promoted against the ratio: the RevOpsAF podcast, because it rides on a commute and so costs almost no attention she has to find, and the RevOps Co-op Slack, which occupies the reserved community slot - the standing floor, not a winner of the ratio. MOPs-Apalooza is costed as days cleared in October, not as ~2 min/week of amortised nothing.

## Negative example (do not deliver this)

**"Top RevOps resources for Sofia (90 min/week)"** - 14 weekly sources: RevOps FM, The Revenue Formula, Ops Cast, RevOpsAF, Growth Unhinged, GTMnow, 6sense, Demandbase, Chili Piper, Gong, Pipeline by ZoomInfo, HockeyStack, r/RevOps, OpsStars newsletter - plus Pavilion listed last, "in case budget frees up". _"Growth Unhinged has 85,000 readers so it's the most authoritative - start there. OpsStars is a well-known ops conference worth joining."_

Why it fails:

- **Blows the budget**: 14 weekly sources is 3+ hours against a 90-minute budget. The list will be abandoned, which is total failure, not partial.
- **Unverified and stale entries presented flat**: RevOps FM and The Revenue Formula were ~7.5 and ~9 months stale at check; r/RevOps was unverifiable. None is labelled.
- **A dead source recommended from memory**: OpsStars' domain is a for-sale parking page. It was "well-known" once; the remembered name was never re-resolved.
- **Audience size mistaken for authority**: 85,000 readers measures reach. The reason to rank Growth Unhinged highly is its named author and benchmark data quality - the count alone justifies nothing.
- **No vendor-run flags**: five vendor blogs presented as neutral sources.
- **A ruled-out source demoted instead of deleted**: Sofia said she cannot pay for a peer community, so Pavilion is not a low-ranked option - it is not an option. Parked at the bottom, it reads as available and comes back every refresh.
- **Ranked flat, then contradicted**: fourteen rows in no stated order, topped by a "start there" justified by a subscriber count. The reader picks row one by default, which is a ranking nobody argued for.
