# Books

Compiled 2026-09-08. Every title confirmed real via Goodreads ratings data - no invented titles, no purchase/affiliate links.

## Foundational

- **Predictable Revenue** - Aaron Ross & Marylou Tyler. The book that named the outbound-pipeline-generation motion RevOps now sits on top of; predates "RevOps" as a term but is the field's most-cited ancestor text.
- **Revenue Operations: A New Way to Align Sales & Marketing, Monetize Data, and Ignite Growth** - Stephen G. Diorio & Chris K. Hummel. The closest thing this field has to a foundational textbook; recurs across nearly every independent "best RevOps books" list found.
- **From Impossible to Inevitable: How Hyper-Growth Companies Create Predictable Revenue** - Aaron Ross & Jason Lemkin. Foundational text on predictable pipeline generation at scale, widely cited (1,235+ ratings).
- **The Sales Acceleration Formula: Using Data, Technology, and Inbound Selling to Go from $0 to $100 Million** - Mark Roberge (former HubSpot CRO). A core text for data-driven sales-ops scaling.

## Practitioner build guides

- **The Revenue Operations Manual: How to Build a High-Growth, Predictable and Scalable Business** - Laura Adint. Practitioner-oriented build guide.
- **The RevOps Pendulum: Finding Stability in Go-to-Market Chaos** - Haris Odobasic. Highest-rated of this set (4.38 average).
- **Revenue Architecture** - Jacco van der Kooij. Direct source for the Bowtie and SPICED frameworks referenced via Winning by Design in sources.md and in people-to-follow.md.
- **Aligned to Achieve: How to Unite Your Sales and Marketing Teams into a Single Force for Growth** - Tracy Eiler. Addresses the sales/marketing alignment problem RevOps exists to solve.

## Adjacent (monetization/pricing-ops)

- **The Pricing Roadmap: How to Design B2B SaaS Pricing Models That Your Customers Will Love** - Ulrik Lehrskov-Schmidt. Monetization and pricing-ops; adjacent to core RevOps but frequently cited alongside it (4.44 average, 206 ratings).
