## Positive example - Senior Analyst targeting a first management seat

Interview answers behind this plan:

- Six years in GTM, four of them in CRM administration at a 400-person B2B enterprise SaaS company, now Senior RevOps Analyst with no reports and no budget.
- Wants the people-manager fork.
- UK-based, remote, will consider hybrid in London.
- Twelve-month timeline.
- Strengths in systems configuration and reporting; actively dislikes running enablement sessions.

```
CAREER PLAN - A. Okonkwo, 2026-08-26, v1

Position   : origin function CRM administration | 6 yrs GTM, 4 in ops
             current level by scope: Analyst rung - 0 reports, no budget, executes
             defined requests; title says "Senior" but scope does not
             motion: B2B enterprise, sales-led

Archetype  : Systems Architect (technical + tactical) | Systems track
             path shape: Monopoly - sequential, credential-driven, 3 moves in 6 yrs
             growth wing per the matrix points at platform/architecture leadership,
             NOT at the people-manager fork the candidate wants -> flagged, see Risks

Target     : Manager rung by scope: owns forecasting workflow end to end, 2-3 reports,
             prioritises across at least two GTM functions
             motion: unchanged (B2B enterprise)
             fork: people manager, chosen deliberately against archetype default
             timeline: 12 months | geography: UK remote, London hybrid acceptable

Gaps       : forecasting ownership | evidence: none - has built forecast reports,
               never owned accuracy | required: one quarter owning forecast accuracy
               against a stated baseline | close by: volunteer for Q4 forecast
               process, agree the metric with the VP Sales in writing | due 2026-11-30
             cross-functional prioritisation | evidence: none - takes requests via
               ticket queue | required: a written intake and prioritisation framework
               adopted by 2+ teams | close by: propose framework, run for one quarter
               | due 2027-01-31
             people management | evidence: none | required: mentoring one junior with
               a documented ramp | close by: ask for the next analyst hire's onboarding
               | due 2027-02-28
             finance fluency | evidence: partial - builds bookings reports, cannot
               model margin | required: can defend a revenue model to a CFO | close by:
               free foundational RevOps course + monthly session with FP&A | due 2027-03-31
             executive communication | evidence: none above department-head level
               | required: one exec-audience presentation delivered | close by: present
               the forecast findings at the quarterly business review | due 2026-12-15

Ledger     : claim "cut forecast variance"
               baseline: 18% variance across FY25 Q1-Q3, from the quarterly reporting pack
               intervention owned: consolidated three next-step fields to one required
                 field with a validation rule; wrote the rule, ran the migration, trained
                 the team over one week - sole owner, no vendor involved
               delta + window: 18% -> 6% over the two quarters following rollout
               provable via: before/after reporting pack, the field audit log, the
                 training deck with dates
             claim "cut territory planning time"
               baseline: 3 weeks of manual planning per cycle, 4 cycles a year
               intervention owned: built the territory model and the assignment logic
               delta + window: 3 weeks -> 2 days per cycle, measured across 2 cycles
               provable via: the model, the two cycle timelines
             claim "improved pipeline hygiene" -> REJECTED, no baseline exists.
               Rewrite once the stale-deal report has run for a full quarter.

Interview  : expected loop: recruiter screen | SQL take-home + deck | cross-functional
               panel (Sales, Marketing, CS) | exec presentation
             readiness: SQL take-home - practised, 2 dry runs under 2 hrs
                        CRM redesign work sample - strong, this is the day job
                        prioritisation role-play - WEAK, no framework to name yet
                        forecast diagnosis - WEAK until the Q4 ownership lands
             story bank: Leadership 1/4 (thin), Problem-Solving 4/4, Collaboration 2/4,
               Achievement 3/4, Failure-Growth 1/4 (thin) - all at 3 lengths
             referees: 2 confirmed and briefed, 1 pending

Comp       : level band UK Manager base £55,000-£85,000 (Live Digital, 2025-26)
             cross-check: RevOps Co-op 2025 report, all-level median $129,155 total (Nov-2024)
             floor £62,000 | target £72,000 | stretch £80,000
             equity: treat as upside only, not counted toward the floor
             market note: UK remote premiums have disappeared (BisonRS, 2026) - do not
               ask for one

Vetting    : mandate - must be written, must include changing process outside the ops team
             reporting line - accept CRO, COO or Finance; a VP Sales line is a walk-away
             owned metric - forecast accuracy, named in the offer conversation
             headcount + budget - 2+ reports, or it is not the Manager rung
             last person in the seat - ask the team, not the recruiter

Roadmap    : 90 days  - own Q4 forecast accuracy with a written baseline; present at QBR;
                        draft the intake framework
             6 months - intake framework adopted by 2 teams; mentoring one junior;
                        prioritisation story banked and rehearsed
             12 months - apply externally at Manager scope; 2 quarters of forecast
                        ownership evidenced; finance fluency demonstrable

Risks      : ticket-taking admin - the current queue is exactly this trap; the intake
               framework is the countermeasure, and if it is refused, that is a signal
               about the employer, not about the candidate
             platform over-specialisation - Systems track depth caps the leadership path;
               finance fluency is the deliberate counterweight
             title over scope - a "Manager" offer with 0 reports is the Analyst rung
               repriced; the Vetting block is the guard
             archetype/fork mismatch - the matrix points elsewhere; revisit at 6 months
               and change the target rather than force it

Review     : monthly against the gap due dates | re-plan if the forecast ownership is
             refused, if the reporting line changes, or on any offer
```

## Negative example - the plan that fails the interview it was built for

```
CAREER PLAN - <candidate>, v1  [DO NOT SHIP]

Target     : Director of Revenue Operations - the title is the goal, company size open
Ledger     : "Drove $4.2M in incremental pipeline through RevOps improvements"
             "Owned the full GTM tech stack"
             "Transformed forecasting across the business"
Roadmap    : Q1 get certified. Q2 apply to Director roles. Q3 negotiate hard.
```

Four failures, each with a cost.

**The revenue claim collapses under one question.** "Drove $4.2M in incremental pipeline" invites an incrementality probe the candidate cannot win, because nearly 90% of B2B SaaS still runs last-touch or basic multi-touch attribution (State of B2B Marketing Attribution 2025, 66 companies, via Unify). The underlying model does not support the claim even if the number is real.

The defensible rewrite isolates the lever actually owned: "routing latency went from 26 hours to under 10 minutes; speed-to-lead is the lever I owned, and pipeline moved alongside it." Cost of not rewriting: the interview stops being about competence and starts being about credibility.

**The stack claim invites the garbage-data follow-up.** "Owned the full GTM tech stack" says nothing verifiable and sets up the classic trap - a polished dashboard whose numbers fall apart when the interviewer asks what data quality it rests on. State the baseline unprompted instead.

**The target is a title, not a scope.** A Director seat at a 30-person company is a hands-on IC role; the same title at 2,000 people implies a team and boardroom altitude. Danielle Marquis puts the same point at VP level: two VP RevOps roles at different scales "are not the same job." Cost: the candidate accepts a title they wanted and a job they did not, then leaves inside a year - which is the turnover pattern RevSearch describes for cross-functional titles with single-function mandates.

**The roadmap has no verifiable milestone.** "Get certified" is an activity, not evidence, and above entry level hands-on experience outweighs credentials anyway. Every roadmap line needs a date, an artifact and a way to prove it happened.

## The contrast in one line

The good plan can be audited by a stranger: every claim has a baseline, an owner, a delta and a place to look it up. The bad plan can only be asserted, which means the first competent interviewer dismantles it.
