# Report shape and worked example

## Section order

Board deck or monthly executive review, main body in this order; validate section by section with the user before finalizing:

1. **The answer** (one page): period verdict vs. plan, the single biggest driver, the response underway, the decision being asked.
2. **Priorities status**: the three to five commitments from last period, each with a plain on-track / at-risk / off-track call.
3. **Spine scorecard**: every spine metric as plan / actual / forecast, same order as last period.
4. **The miss or the beat, decomposed**: movement bridge, cause at stated confidence, fix, owner, proof metric.
5. **Forward look**: next-period forecast with its confidence basis (coverage, cohort trend), reset targets if any.
6. **Decisions**: each with context and a recommendation - never an open question with no proposed answer.
7. **Appendix**: full bridges, cohort tables, segment cuts, definitions changelog.

## Good vs. bad opening

**Bad** (a tour, not an answer): "Q3 Revenue Overview. This quarter we closed 47 deals across three segments. Slide 2 shows monthly recurring revenue by month; slide 3 shows pipeline by stage; slide 4 shows win rate by region..." Ten slides in, the reader still does not know whether the quarter was good.

**Good** (the whole story on page one): "We ended Q3 at $30.7M ARR, $0.9M under the $31.6M plan. The miss is entirely new-logo bookings - retention held at plan. Root cause is confirmed: pipeline created in Q2 was 40% below target, so Q3 started under-covered. The fix (two demand programs reallocated, started week 2 of Q3) shows in Q4 pipeline, but Q4 coverage is still 2.4x against the 3.1x our own close rates require, so we forecast $31.9M ending ARR against the $33.4M plan and are asking the board to approve the revised Q4 target and the $400K demand-generation reallocation."

## Worked example - quarterly board revenue section, with a miss

Fictional B2B subscription company; all numbers illustrative and internally consistent.

**Page one - the answer.** As the good opening above. Verdict, driver, response, ask, in four sentences.

**Spine scorecard** (plan / actual / forecast next period):

| Metric                                   | Plan      | Actual    | Next-period forecast     |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------- | --------- | ------------------------ |
| Ending ARR                               | $31.6M    | $30.7M    | $31.9M (plan was $33.4M) |
| Net-new ARR                              | $3.2M     | $2.3M     | $1.2M                    |
| NRR (trailing-12)                        | 110%      | 108%      | 108%                     |
| GRR (trailing-12)                        | 91%       | 91%       | 91%                      |
| Pipeline coverage (next qtr, unweighted) | 3.1x      | 2.4x      | -                        |
| Win rate (decided deals, count / dollar) | 24% / 27% | 25% / 21% | -                        |

**The miss, decomposed** (movement bridge; offsets shown, not netted):

| Movement       | Plan       | Actual     | Gap        |
| -------------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Beginning ARR  | $28.4M     | $28.4M     | -          |
| New business   | +$2.6M     | +$1.9M     | -$0.7M     |
| Expansion      | +$1.1M     | +$1.1M     | -          |
| Reactivation   | +$0.1M     | +$0.1M     | -          |
| Contraction    | -$0.3M     | -$0.3M     | -          |
| Churn          | -$0.3M     | -$0.5M     | -$0.2M     |
| **Ending ARR** | **$31.6M** | **$30.7M** | **-$0.9M** |

Narrative under the table: "$0.7M of the gap is new business - confirmed cause is Q2 pipeline creation, 40% under target, which left Q3 starting at 2.1x coverage against the 3.1x our own history requires (root-cause detail: separate diagnostic, summarized in appendix). $0.2M is churn: two logos, both flagged at-risk two quarters ago, both in the segment we are deprioritizing - not a new pattern. Note the dollar-weighted win rate fell to 21% while count-based held at 25%: we are winning smaller deals, which is consistent with the under-covered enterprise pipeline, and is the number to watch next quarter."

**Forward look**: "Q4 forecast is $31.9M ending ARR at moderate confidence: coverage is 2.4x against a 3.1x requirement, so the forecast already assumes below-plan new business; retention assumptions are unchanged and have held for four quarters. Proof metric for the demand fix: Q4-created pipeline, reported at the next meeting against a $9.5M target."

**Decisions**: "1. Approve revised Q4 ending-ARR target of $31.9M (recommendation: approve; holding the original target would force pull-forward behavior that costs Q1). 2. Approve $400K reallocation from field events to the two demand programs showing 3x the pipeline-per-dollar in our own last-two-quarter data (recommendation: approve)."

Why this works:

- The miss leads.
- Nothing is netted or smoothed.
- The churn offset is shown separately from the bookings gap.
- The cause carries a confidence label with the investigation referenced rather than replayed.
- Every forward number states its basis.
- Both asks arrive with a recommendation.

The board's first question is a decision, not "wait, how do you define NRR?"
