## Planning consequences

- **The objective ceiling is the real differentiator.** On LinkedIn the format cannot carry a conversion objective, which forces the sequenced handoff design in SKILL.md. On Meta and TikTok the same mechanic runs straight to purchase - which is why founder-led consumer brands treat it as a performance channel rather than a funnel stage.
- **Permission duration differs structurally.** A per-video code with a fixed term is a licence with an expiry date to track and renew. An approve/revoke toggle is consent that can vanish mid-flight. Plan continuity differently for each.
- **Employment tie (LinkedIn only):** the featured person must list the sponsoring company as a current employer or collaborator, and the ad is bound to the parent company page. Departure breaks eligibility and the creative cannot be re-hosted. Non-employees became eligible (with consent and partnership labelling) on 1 April 2024.
- **No cross-network efficiency ranking is offered here, deliberately.** The featured person's audience exists on one or two networks and nowhere else, so presence decides the network before any value-per-effort comparison would - ranking the rows would be false precision.
  - Where a person is genuinely active on both a full-objective and a trust-layer network, rank by handoff work: efficiency full-objective (Meta, TikTok) > trust-layer (LinkedIn). The full-objective network pays back inside the flight, while the trust-layer one makes the downstream conversion stage a standing job.
  - Value inverts that wherever the buying committee only exists on the professional network, the ordinary B2B enterprise case.
- **Two networks simply do not offer this.** A plan naming YouTube or Reddit for this format is wrong, not merely suboptimal - route those channels to standard brand campaigns instead.
- **EEA specifics (LinkedIn):** targeting based on group membership was disabled in the EEA in 2024 because it could reveal sensitive-category data. Sponsored messaging requires explicit consent in the EU. Factor both into audience design for EEA-inclusive campaigns.
