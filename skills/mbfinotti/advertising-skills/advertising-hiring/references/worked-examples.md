# Worked Examples

Both examples are this skill's own illustrative constructions, grounded in the sourced patterns; the companies are fictional.

## Positive: first dedicated hire at a DTC brand

Context from the Interview:

- DTC skincare brand, $110k/month across two paid channels currently run by the founder.
- Measurement "partly" trusted.
- B2C.
- Panel of founder plus head of brand, two rounds possible.

- **Rung decision:** performance marketer. First dedicated hire, measurement not validated, spend past the ~$50k/month dedicated-hire breakpoint, a third channel planned. Not a media buyer - nobody owns measurement yet; not a growth marketer - the mandate stops at paid plus measurement, not lifecycle.
- **Outcomes (ranked, dated):**
  1. Conversion tracking validated with platform-versus-backend reconciliation documented, by day 45.
  2. Creative-testing cadence live with win/loss documentation, by day 60.
  3. Blended MER held above the agreed break-even floor while spend scales 30%, by day 120 - deliberately outside the first 90 days, because learning phases and attribution validation cannot resolve faster.
- **Loop (2 rounds):**
  - Screen (30 minutes): context-fit questions, the kill-decision question, the resume's "$400k/month managed" spend claim paired with an incrementality-versus-attribution probe.
  - Case round (90 minutes): a 60-minute live screen-share audit of a sanitized account copy, followed by a ROAS-drop scenario whose dataset is deliberately inconclusive - naming the ambiguity scores top marks. The founder joins the last 30 minutes for the five-year question and the metrics-cadence question.
- **Scorecard:**
  - Five competencies, each with behavioral anchors written before the first interview.
  - Scores cite evidence.
  - Weights labelled as this skill's convention.
- **Ramp:**
  - All access granted day one.
  - A $500/day self-serve change threshold set in week one.
  - Co-sign through week 6.
  - Independent within $3k/day by day 60.
  - Full authority at day 90, after the measurement audit passes.

  First win targeted: the tracking reconciliation, not a scaling bet. Watch areas:
  - Retreating into the ad platforms instead of finishing the measurement audit.
  - Creative briefs stalling because no one owns the designer relationship.

## Negative: the scoping mis-hire, with its cost

Context: B2B SaaS, no channels live, no tracking, six-month sales cycle. The company hires an ex-DTC media buyer whose resume leads with "$1M/month managed," after one 45-minute interview of platform-features questions and no work sample.

What went wrong, mapped to the evidence:

- The spend screen was unpaired - no kill-decision or incrementality probe - so nobody learned the $1M was the execution of someone else's strategy (the platform-operator-without-judgment pattern).
- A first-hire generalist brief went to a channel specialist who had never stood up measurement from zero.
- DTC instincts optimized toward first-order conversion on a six-month pipeline - the documented non-transferable-instincts failure, in the exact direction the sources warn about.
- No spend threshold was set and access trickled in over two weeks, so the ramp stalled exactly the way the onboarding evidence predicts.
- The hire was let go at month three - inside the 3-6 month window in which a channel verdict is not yet possible, and inside the ramp window where churn points at scoping and onboarding, not the candidate.

Cost: a year of salary-equivalent spend between hiring, severance, and re-hiring - plus damage that outlives the person, because "your account history makes recovery harder even after you fix the problem" (The Media Captain). Every failure above was visible at scoping time; none required a better candidate.
