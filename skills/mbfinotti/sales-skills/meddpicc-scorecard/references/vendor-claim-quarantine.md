# Vendor claim quarantine

MEDDPICC circulates alongside a body of outcome figures published by the parties selling MEDDPICC tooling and training. They are correlational at best and self-reported at worst. Quarantine them: never present one as an expected outcome, and if you quote one at all, label it vendor marketing.

## Never present as expected outcomes

- "18% higher win rates / 24% larger deals / 26% shorter sales cycles" - repeated across MEDDPICC-tooling vendors with no independent citation.
- "Forecast accuracy to 95%" and similar forecast-accuracy transformations - self-reported by parties selling MEDDPICC software or training.
- The methodology publisher's own homepage claims ("increase deal size by 62%", "double your win rate") - published by the vendor selling the training.
- "40% higher close rates", "2.4x/3.1x multi-threading multipliers", "78% of accounts single-threaded" - widely blogged, but not traceable to the Ebsta/Pavilion reports they are attributed to.
- Enterprise post-yes stall figures (~27-28% ranges) - circulate through vendor-adjacent channels only. No analyst firm (Gartner, Forrester, CSO Insights, RAIN Group) publishes a figure isolating this specific post-verbal-yes stage as its own metric. Cite the sourced analogs below instead of this range.

## When a figure is in neither list

Default to the quarantine. State the deal-side mechanic instead of the number: "single-threaded deals are fragile because the evidence for every letter runs through one person who can leave" carries the argument without borrowing a multiplier nobody can trace.
