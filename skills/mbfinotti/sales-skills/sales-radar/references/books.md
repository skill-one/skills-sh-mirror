# Books

Foundational and recent B2B sales books, confirmed by title/author/year. No purchase or affiliate links - a book's title and author are enough to find it through whatever channel the user prefers.

## Foundational

- **SPIN Selling** - Neil Rackham (1988). The root reference for modern discovery frameworks: Situation-Problem-Implication-Need-payoff questioning for large-account sales.
- **The Challenger Sale** - Matthew Dixon & Brent Adamson (2011). The standard reference for complex B2B selling: "teach, tailor, take control" insight-selling.
- **New Sales. Simplified.** - Mike Weinberg (2012). New-business/prospecting fundamentals for reps building pipeline from zero.
- **Cracking the Sales Management Code** - Jason Jordan (2012). Connects sales activities to metrics a manager can actually coach against.

## Negotiation and discovery

- **Never Split the Difference** - Chris Voss & Tahl Raz (2016). Tactical empathy applied to deal-closing and pricing conversations; see Black Swan Group in sources.md for the same author's cross-ecosystem podcast/blog.
- **Gap Selling** - Keenan (2018). Problem-centric selling: diagnose the current-vs-future-state gap before pitching.
- **MEDDICC** - Andy Whyte (2020). Enterprise/complex-sales qualification methodology, same brand as Masters of MEDDICC in sources.md's podcast table.

## Recent

- **The JOLT Effect** - Matthew Dixon & Ted McKenna (2022). Addresses buyer indecision as the primary deal-killer; a Challenger follow-up.
- **Sell Without Selling Out** - Andy Paul (2022). An authenticity-first counter to high-pressure tactics.

Every title above is foundational or well-cited enough to survive a fast market; none is B2C-specific. If the user's goal is B2C high-ticket craft, treat this list as B2B-transferable principle, not a vertical match - no equivalent B2C-specific book canon was verified in this pass.
