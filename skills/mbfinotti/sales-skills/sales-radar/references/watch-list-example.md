# Watch list examples

One worked example of the right output shape, then the tempting wrong shape. Both draw on the 2026-08-26 snapshot in the sources reference - the positive example shows how much of that snapshot should survive filtering (very little), and the negative example shows what happens when it all does.

## Worked example - the right shape

Interview answers behind it:

- Role: mid-market B2B SaaS AE.
- Deals: ~$40k ACV, 6-8 week cycles.
- Time budget: one hour a week.
- Medium: mostly podcast audio on the commute, plus one email newsletter.
- Already follows: 30 Minutes to President's Club.
- Current goal: discovery and negotiation.

### Your sales radar - mid-market SaaS AE, ~1 hour/week

Built 2026-08-26 from the 2026-08-26 source snapshot. Six sources; anything more would blow your hour.

Ordering note: the default puts newsletters and benchmark reports ahead of podcasts on attention efficiency. Your commute reverses that - audio costs you time you were spending anyway, so a weekly podcast leads this list and a second newsletter did not make it.

**Sharpen discovery (core, weekly - ~40 min)**

1. **Make It Happen Mondays** - John Barrows (JB Sales). Podcast, weekly. Best for: AE-level discovery and fundamentals; the rare trainer r/sales vouches for as non-guru. Fits your commute slot. Status: `verified-active` (org site; episode dates live on the podcast feed, not the website). Last verified 2026-08-26.
2. **Leslie Venetz's newsletter** - Substack, 2-3x/week; read one per week. Best for: discovery and outbound messaging craft you can lift into your own emails. Your one email subscription. Status: `verified-active` (posts through Aug 24 2026). Last verified 2026-08-26.

**Level up negotiation (rotate in - ~15 min)**

3. **Chris Voss / Black Swan Group** - articles and podcast, irregular cadence. Best for: negotiation technique; one of the few sources that genuinely serves both B2B and B2C. Dip in when a deal reaches pricing. Status: `verified-active` (undated but current site). Last verified 2026-08-26.

**Stay current on the market (monthly - ~5 min)**

4. **The GTM Newsletter (GTMnow)** - weekly; skim once a month. Best for: where the broader GTM world is moving, without living on LinkedIn. Status: `verified-active` (posts through Aug 25 2026). Last verified 2026-08-26.
5. **RepVue Cloud Sales Index** - quarterly. Best for: a reality check on quota attainment and comp before your next review cycle (Q4 2025: 43.83% attainment). Status: `verified-active` (mid-2026 update). Last verified 2026-08-26.

**Peer sanity check (as needed)**

6. **r/sales** - community. Best for: unfiltered practitioner consensus and gut checks on tools or advice; not a tactics source. Status: `unverified` - the platform blocks automated checks, so this is a tooling limitation, not a health signal; widely reported active. Last checked 2026-08-26.

Deliberately excluded: **30MPC** (you already follow it - keep it as your anchor), **Outbound Squad** (overlaps Barrows for your current goal; revisit if you move upmarket into outbound-heavy territory), **Pavilion** (paid and leadership-oriented; worth it when you manage reps).

What an hour a week cannot buy: a community you actually participate in, and a conference. Both rank top on value and bottom on efficiency, so a minutes budget never reaches them. Revisit both the moment your goal becomes a comp negotiation or a move up - at that point they carry information no newsletter publishes, and a conference gets budgeted as two days once a year rather than out of this hour.

**Coverage:** 6 sources across 4 mediums (podcast, newsletter, articles, community) plus 1 quarterly benchmark. Estimated cost ~60 min/week against your 1-hour budget. Snapshot 2026-08-26: 5 of 6 `verified-active`, 1 `unverified` (labeled above). Suggested re-check: quarterly, starting with the unverified entry.

## Negative example - the wrong shape

The same interview answers can tempt an agent into producing this. Everything below is wrong even though most individual names are real:

> ### The Ultimate Sales Resource List - 40+ sources every rep needs!
>
> **Podcasts:** 30 Minutes to President's Club, Make It Happen Mondays, Sales Gravy (470,000 listeners!), Outbound Squad (50k subscribers), The Advanced Selling Podcast, Revenue Builders, Topline, The Sales Leadership Podcast, Masters of MEDDICC, SDR Chronicles, Win Rate, The Brutal Truth About Sales, The D2D Podcast, Stay Paid…
>
> **Newsletters:** GTMnow, Sales Hacker, Leslie Venetz, Josh Braun's Substack, Kyle Coleman's Substack…
>
> **People:** Jason Bay, Grant Cardone, Andy Elliott, Jeremy Miner, Ian Koniak, Tom Ferry…
>
> All of these are active and highly recommended - subscribe to as many as you can!

Why this fails:

- **It's a catalogue, not a watch list.** Forty-plus sources for a user with one hour a week is a filtering job handed back to the person who asked you to do the filtering.
- **It resurrects sources the snapshot already ruled out.** SDR Chronicles, Sales Hacker, Josh Braun's and Kyle Coleman's Substacks, Masters of MEDDICC and Andy Elliott all sit in the snapshot's graveyard and appear in no live table. Pulling them back up as recommendations is exactly what the graveyard exists to prevent, and "all of these are active" is false.
- **It has no ordering at all**, so the reader picks by row position. Even the ranked version of this failure would be wrong if it led with the cheapest rows: six newsletters fit any budget and would still miss the negotiation coaching this user asked for.
- **It invents and misattributes numbers.** "470,000 listeners" conflates Sales Gravy's newsletter subscriber claim with its podcast audience (not stated); "50k subscribers" for Outbound Squad appears nowhere in the snapshot - it was made up to look authoritative.
- **It mixes the two ecosystems into one flat list.** Tom Ferry, Andy Elliott, and The D2D Podcast are B2C high-ticket sources with near-zero relevance to a mid-market SaaS AE, filed next to 30MPC as if interchangeable.
- **It ignores what the user already follows** (30MPC is recommended back to them), **ignores their goal** (nothing is tied to discovery or negotiation), and **carries no statuses, no last-verified dates, no snapshot date** - so it starts rotting silently the moment it's delivered.
- **It hides source character.** Grant Cardone and Andy Elliott are primarily course-sellers; recommending them without saying so misrepresents what the user's time buys.
